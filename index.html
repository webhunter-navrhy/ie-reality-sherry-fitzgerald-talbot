A Spectacular and Rare Irish Castle on Approx. 20 Acres of Private Woodlands.Tucked deep within the tranquil beauty of the Irish Midlands, Emmel Castle is one of County Offaly's most striking Historic Estates. A harmonious blend of Medieval fortification and Georgian refinement, this remarkable property includes a 15th-century Tower House and a graceful 17th-Century Country House—set on approximately 20 acres of private woodlands and gardens, in an area renowned for its scenic charm and historic depth.PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS • Accommodation: Tower House + Country House• Bedrooms: 3+• Reception Rooms: 3• Bathrooms: 3• Land: 20 acres (Woodland, Gardens, Courtyard, Yard, Outbuildings)• Location: 10 mins to Cloughjordan, 1.5 hrs to Dublin, 1 hr to Galway & Shannon Airport• Status: Listed National Monument (SMR)RICH IN HISTORY & CHARACTERFirst constructed around 1450 as a stronghold of the O'Carroll Clan, Emmel Castle once stood among 25 castles under O'Carroll ownership across the region. In 1683, the elegant country house was added by “Long Anthony O'Carroll,” famously close to seven feet tall. Following the Cromwellian conquest, the estate was confiscated but later restored to the family during the monarchy's return—eventually leaving O'Carroll ownership in the 18th century.From the battlements, rising 90 feet, panoramic views stretch across Offaly, Limerick, Clare, Galway, and Tipperary.THE BUILDINGSTower House (15th Century)o Rectangular-plan with circular stair turret (N-E angle)o Pitched slate roof with castellated parapets and bartizanso Stone chimneystacks, loop openings, corbels, mural passageso Defensive machicolations, castellated porcho Historic Great Hall, Music Room, and Art Studioo Master Bedroom, Family Bathroomo All rooms with period fireplaces and latticed windowsCountry House (1683 with 19th-century alterations)o Pitched slate roof, terracotta ridge tiles, rendered chimneystackso Timber sash windows with stucco surroundso Round-headed and square-headed door openings with stained glass, pilasters, fanlightso Elaborate ceiling plasterwork in hallwayo Drawing Room & Living Room with historic fireplaceso Kitchen, Butler's Pantry, Laundry (Garden Level)o Two Double Bedrooms, Two Bathrooms (First Floor)OUTBUILDINGS & FEATURES• Historic stone outbuildings with slate roofs• Walled garden to the south-west of the house• Courtyard accessed by wrought iron gates• Stone and brick outbuilding dated 1883• Ashlar gate piers with carved caps and sweeping boundary walls• Bellcote, date plaques, external stairs, and timber/metal doors with Gothic and Classical detailingLOCATION & LIFESTYLESet in a region steeped in heritage and natural beauty, Emmel Castle offers an exceptional rural lifestyle.
